PRSNL Digital Stationery & Stylus Notebook

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2026 • Effective Date: September 21, 2026

In Plain English (Privacy by Design)

PRSNL is an offline-first notes application. Your handwriting, vector stylus strokes, drawings, audio, and PDF markups are stored locally on your device in sandboxed application storage. We do not sell your personal notes, we do not train AI models on your handwriting, and writing statistics (such as stylus distance and yearly wrapped) are computed 100% on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the PRSNL mobile and tablet application (referred to as "PRSNL", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), created and maintained by Param Khodiyar. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of your personal writings, drawings, documents, and device information.

2. Data We Process & Where It Stays

2.1 Handwritten Notes, Vector Ink & Documents (100% Local by Default)

All notes created within PRSNL—including pen strokes, pencil sketches, highlighter marks, geometric shapes, STEM graphs, and text boxes—are stored directly on your physical device using our proprietary vector file format and a local SQLite database (Room). This content is sandboxed within the application directory.

  • We cannot read, access, or analyze the content of your notes.
  • Your handwriting is never scanned, monetized, or fed into machine learning training pipelines.

2.2 PDF Documents & Annotations

When you import PDF documents into PRSNL, they are processed and rendered entirely on-device using our embedded C++ Pdfium rendering engine. We do not transmit your PDF files or annotations to any remote servers for rendering or analysis.

2.3 Writing Distance & "Notes Wrapped" Statistics

PRSNL calculates metrics such as meters written, stroke counts, and writing streaks for your personal insights and the Yearly Wrapped feature. This aggregation takes place strictly at write time on your device's local database (in the local writing_stats table). None of your usage statistics or writing velocity data is sent to external ad networks or third-party analytics.

2.4 Locked Folders & PIN Security

If you protect folders using a numeric PIN or security questions, your credentials and answers are hashed on-device using cryptographic one-way hashes. Neither PRSNL nor any third party possesses a master key or backdoor to access your locked folders.

3. Optional Features & Third-Party Services

3.1 Optional Cloud Synchronization (Google Account)

If you intentionally sign in with Google to enable multi-device sync, PRSNL uses Google Identity Services and Firebase Authentication (Google Cloud) to authenticate your identity. Your synchronized notebooks are stored securely in Google Cloud Storage and Firestore in your private account workspace solely to replicate your notes across your personal devices. You may disable sync or sign out at any time.

3.2 In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (PRSNL Pro)

Subscription and license transactions are processed directly through Google Play In-App Billing. We do not receive, collect, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.

3.3 Crash Logs & Diagnostics

Crash logs recorded by our internal diagnostic utility (CrashLogger) remain stored in your device's local cache. They are never uploaded automatically; they can only be reviewed or manually exported if you explicitly choose to inspect the system log.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

PRSNL is designed as a productivity and educational stationery notebook for users of all ages, including students. Because we do not collect personal data or track users, PRSNL complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international children's privacy standards.

5. Data Retention, Export & Deletion Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You retain full ownership and control over your data:

  • Export: You can export notebooks and annotated PDFs at any time as vector PDFs or shareable image summaries.
  • Deletion: Deleting a page, notebook, or folder immediately deletes the associated records and files from your device storage. If cloud sync is active, deletion propagates to your cloud storage container.
  • Complete App Reset: Uninstalling PRSNL or clearing app storage from Android settings completely purges all local data, database records, and cached files.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new capabilities or platform standards. Any revisions will be accompanied by an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
